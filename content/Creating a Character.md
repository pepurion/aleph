Before creating a character take a look in [[Character Data]].

1. Imagine your character, have a base idea from what you are building
2. Pick a Species, take note of their characteristics
	- Block and Dodge ratings as they are
	- Con, End, Eff
	- Gifted skills
	- Additional info
3. You are level 10 - so have 15 SP, 10 TP and 1 Feat
4. Choose Talents, generally focus on a few, don't spread your TP to too many trees
5. Distribute SP, mind the limits and gifted skills
6. Choose a Feat
7. Identify your character
	- Choose a name
	- Add basic info to your info tab
	- Maybe some flavor text and story
