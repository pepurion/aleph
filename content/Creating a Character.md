Before creating a character take a look in [[Character Data]].

1. Imagine your character, have a base idea from what you are building
2. Pick a Species, take note of their characteristics
	- Block and Dodge dice values as they are
	- Con, End, Eff
	- Gifted skills
	- Additional limitations or abilities, if they have them
3. You are level 10 (it's for your own good)
	- Have 15 SP, 10 TP and 1 Feat
	- Until next level - 1000xp
4. Choose Talents
	- Write Talents and their costs down
	- Generally focus on a few trees, don't spread your TP to too many
5. Distribute SP
	- Mind the limits and gifted skills
	- Write down the values and their modifiers next to them
6. Choose a Feat
7. Identify your character
	- Choose a name
	- Add basic info to your info tab
	- Maybe some flavor text and backstory
