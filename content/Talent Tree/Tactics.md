The Tactics tree offers evasive maneuvers and technical outfitting. The talents in this tree push superior **movement** and reflexive **defense**, including not only increasing one's blocking and dodging capabilities, but also offering alternative methods.

---

| Name        | Effect                                                                                                         | Cost                     |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Sprint**  | When you move, you may spend 3 Stamina to add your Stunt score to your Land Travel for the rest of the action. | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 1 Soul |
| **Athlete** | You may spend 4 Stamina to move using RP as if they were AP.                                                   | 1 Body / 0 Mind / 2 Soul |
| **Parkour** | When you receive kinetic force damage, the damage is halved down.                                              | 2 Body / 0 Mind / 3 Soul |

---

| Name            | Effect                                                                             | Cost                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Reflexive**   | Choose either Block Rating or Dodge Rating. The chosen rating increases by 1 die.  | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 2 Soul |
| **Reflexive**   | Depending on the previous choice, the other rating increases by 1 die.             | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 2 Soul |
| **Illusive**    | Choose either Block Rating or Dodge Rating. The chosen rating increases by 2 dice. | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 4 Soul |
| **Illusive**    | Depending on the previous choice, the other rating increases by 2 dice.            | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 4 Soul |
| **Total Poise** | Your Dodge Rating increases by 2d12 and Block Rating increases by 2d4              | 0 Body / 0 Mind / 7 Soul |
