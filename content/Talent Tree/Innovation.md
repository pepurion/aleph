The Innovation tree is purely in the heart of **craft**. One cannot craft powerful artifacts without first accepting these talents. Innovation not only allows the creation of custom **equipment**, but also advanced usage for said items as well as others, including dismantling and **medical** aid.

---

| Name              | Effect                                                                                                                                               | Cost                     |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Deconstructor** | You may spend 5 minutes at a T1 tool station to destroy an item other than component, receiving half of the components used to craft it, round down. | 0 Body / 1 Mind / 1 Soul |
| **Alchemist**     | When you craft a Consumable, you craft twice as many.                                                                                                | 0 Body / 2 Mind / 2 Soul |
| **Tenacious**     | You may craft T1 items without a tool station. Items and components crafted this way require twice as long and any 1 additional component to craft.  | 0 Body / 4 Mind / 3 Soul |
