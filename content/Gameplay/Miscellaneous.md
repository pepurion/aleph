## Resting
To sleep and satisfy your hunger or any other need, you should stop for a bit and rest. The space around should be safe and comfortable enough for a fulfilling rest.

### Short Rest
A short halt to regain energy and fulfil your needs, be it eating, cleaning your weapon and/or changing oil. Lasts for 1-2 hours. 
**Restore half SHP and all Stamina.**

### Long Rest
A long stop to recover from a hard day, you may sleep, reflect on your life choices and/or analyze all the data you've gathered. Lasts for 6-8 hours. 
**Remove one Fatigue point, and fully restore SHP and Stamina**

## Travel
Creature movement. That's it.

- **Land Travel** - equal to Speed score
- **Water Travel** - equal to Speed score halved up
- **Air Travel** - is 0 unless otherwise stated

## Dying *(Not Advised)*
Upon reaching **0 DHP** a character is considered dying and their body enters **coma**. They also roll on a [[Wound table]].

When a creature's DHP is reduced to 0, further damage will force it's current DHP into the **negatives**. Every minute in coma the body suffers 1 damage to DHP, and when it hits it's maximum in the negatives, the body is **destroyed** and the creature is **dead**.

When the creature is **healed back** into positive DHP, it suffers 3 points of Fatigue and remains at 0 Stamina.

## Size Categories
All creatures and objects exist within a specific size category.

### Miniscule
Fits in a 5cm cube. Slot modifier - x0.5.

### Tiny
Fits in a 25cm cube, but not in a 5cm cube. Slot modifier - x1.

### Small
Fits in a 1m cube, but not in a 25cm cube. Slot modifier - x2.

### Medium
Fits in a 3m cube, but not in a 1m cube. Slot modifier - x5.

### Large
Fits in a 10cm cube, but not in a 3m cube. Slot modifier - x15.

### Huge
Fits in a 40m cube, but not in a 10m cube. Slot modifier - x25.

### Massive
Fits in a 200m cube, but not in a 40m cube. Slot modifier - x50.

### Colossal
Does not fit in a 200m cube. Slot modifier - x100.
