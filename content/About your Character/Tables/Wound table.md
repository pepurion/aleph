Whenever you gain a Wound, roll **d20 with advantage** and adjust according to the type of Damage:

| Roll  | Effect              | Description                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 20-16 | Minor Wound         | Superficial cuts, bruises, or soreness. No lasting effects, minor pain or cosmetic damage                                    |
| 15-11 | Moderate Injury     | Sprains, small lacerations or bleeding. Reduced mobility or slight blood loss until treated                                  |
| 10-5  | Severe Injury       | Fractures, deep cuts, or heavy bleeding. Broken bones, large blood loss, requires urgent medical attention                   |
| 4-2   | Major Wound         | Crushed limbs, severed body parts, or organ damage. Massive blood loss, limb unusable or lost, life threatening if untreated |
| 1     | Catastrophic Injury | Fatal wound, extreme gore, imminent death unless immediate medical care is given                                             |
