Merchants won’t always be there to provide you with what you need, or maybe you just love experimenting. Crafting allows you to take raw materials and combine them into something that will assist in your survival.

---
## Tiers and Rarities
All items are divided into rarities - **Crude, Common, Enhanced, Advanced, Elite, Prime**. Rarity represents how complex and/or powerful the item is. Any weapon, piece of armor, gadget, consumable should have a rarity. If you want to craft, repair or even modify equipment of whatever rarity, you need to have a respective [[Innovation|Talent]].

Crafting itself comes in tiers.

- **T0** - you craft with your bare hands and preferably a surface, you may craft **Crude** items this way
- **T1** - this requires a tool station of some sort, based on what type of equipment you make, you may craft **Common** and **Enhanced** items and components
- **T2** - at this point you need some sort of fabrication device, it won't do the job for you, but it will make the finer adjustments, you may craft **Advanced** and **Elite** items and components
- **T3** - now this is where heavy machinery is needed, not literally, you may craft **Prime** items and components

### Spending Time
Different rarities require varying amount of time to craft.

|           | Items    | Components |

## Components
Components are elements required to craft any item. They come in 3 general categories - **Material**, **Refinement** and **Power** components. Different varieties of items will require different amount of each.

Depending on the nature of components, they can be used in certain fields of crafting, you cannot build a bandage out of solid steel. Most of components are generic and do not posses any effects by themselves, but some may.

Components may be crafted, harvested or foraged.

### Material Components
**MC** are used to build the **structural** parts of items. These are the most abundant form of components and the most common ones used in crafting. The more powerful they are, the more durable and versatile they are.

#### Crafting
Material components can generally be crafted by combining lesser materials into more powerful alloy or composite.

When crafting a component, an Engineering check is made. Upon fail, all components are destroyed. Crude materials cannot be used.

- **Enhanced** Material
	- 2 Common Material
	- Diff 12 Engineering check
- **Advanced** Material
	- 2 Enhanced Material
	- Diff 18 Engineering check
- **Elite** Material
	- 2 Advanced Material
	- Diff 24 Engineering check
- **Prime** Material
	- 2 Elite Material
	- Diff 30 Engineering check

### Refinement Components
**RC** are small stuff that makes an item lighter, smaller or more efficient. This might be precious metals, alchemical substrates or even nanotechnology.

#### Crafting
Refinement components are created by engineering material components artificially.

When crafting a component, an Engineering check is made. Upon fail, all components are destroyed. Crude materials cannot be used.

- **Enhanced** Refinement
	- 1 Common Material, 1 Enhanced Material
	- Diff 14 Engineering check
- **Advanced** Refinement
	- 1 Enhanced Material, 1 Advanced Material
	- Diff 20 Engineering check
- **Elite** Refinement
	- 1 Advanced Material, 1 Elite Material
	- Diff 26 Engineering check
- **Prime** Refinement
	- 1 Elite Material, 1 Prime Material
	- Diff 32 Engineering check

### Power Components
**PC** are the rarest form of component and are not seen in many items, especially in more common ones. They produce or store usable energy to power larger systems. This might be bio- or liquid fuel, power cells, coils, or combustion chambers.

#### Crafting
Power components are manufactured using other components of the same rarity.

When crafting a component, an Engineering check is made. Upon fail, all components are destroyed. Crude materials cannot be used.

- **Common** Power
	- 1 Common Material, 1 Common Refinement
	- Diff 11 Engineering check
- **Enhanced** Power
	- 1 Enhanced Material, 1 Enhanced Refinement
	- Diff 17 Engineering check
- **Advanced** Power
	- 1 Advanced Material, 1 Advanced Refinement
	- Diff 23 Engineering check
- **Elite** Power
	- 1 Elite Material, 1 Elite Refinement
	- Diff 29 Engineering check
- **Prime** Power
	- 1 Prime Material, 1 Prime Refinement
	- Diff 35 Engineering check

### Foraging
This is the act of spending a long period of time searching for something specific. The searches are thorough and include looking above and beneath the surface when available.

Whenever you wish to forage for components, you choose how much time you wish to spend and make a (usually) **Awareness** check. You may spend from 1 to 24 hours, the more time you spend, the higher chance you have. The area should theoretically contain the resource you are looking for, for a successful search.

Upon a **successful** check, you get a random amount of the resource. If you roll a value two times the required difficulty, the time you spend is halved. On **fail**, you spend the full time, but no resource is found.

## Items
Items have multiple categories you choose to craft. Every item has it's own specific modifier limits.

- **Weapon**
	- Fast
	- Moderate
	- Slow
- **Armor**
	- Shield
	- Chest
	- Head
	- Hand
	- Foot
- **Usable**
	- Consumables
	- Gadgets

When crafting an item, you may choose features it will have and the DM (me) will tailor it based on your choice (I don't want to give you the full power, yet). The rarity of an item depends on what rarity components were used in crafting. Higher rarity components can be used in place of lower ones, but not otherwise.

**Gadgets** are special. They can have virtually any functionality. This is the "etc." of the categories.
