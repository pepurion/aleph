As you venture, you may choose how your character adapts to the conditions using talents. **Talent trees** are broken into **branches** and those are further broken into **tiers**.
Branches are separate from each other, but to get talents from higher tiers, you have to get all the prerequisite tier talents from the same branch.

## Core Talents
All players have access to core talent trees.

### Warfare
[[Warfare|Open tree]]
The Warfare tree is a path toward combat and finesse in conflict. Talents from this tree are **battle oriented**, both general and specialized. It is the fine art of not only hitting things really hard, but also hitting things very quickly and in close succession.
